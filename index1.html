Weight Scan v4

One tare + five product weights · lb → g

1. Scan handwritten notes

Photos are automatically oriented using phone image metadata, resized, and enhanced for blue handwriting. If text is sideways, the reader tests multiple rotations. Recognition needs an internet connection. If scanning fails, the app will show which component could not load. Always verify every number.

2. Verify pounds

Always compare all six values with the photograph. Handwriting recognition is experimental and may confuse digits.

3. Results in grams

Tare = tare × 453.59237. Each product result = (product + tare) × 453.59237. Round final values to two decimal places.
