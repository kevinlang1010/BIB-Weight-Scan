Weight Scan v6 beta

Handwriting-focused scanner • Six readings • Grams to two decimals

1. Choose a photo

Photos stay in your browser. The handwriting model is downloaded separately on first use.

2. Read each handwritten number

Tap the center of one handwritten number in the photo. Adjust the crop if needed, then tap Recognize selected. Repeat for tare and each product.

Crop width 36% of photo width

Crop height 17% of photo height

Select a photo, then tap a handwritten number. The model is not loaded until you press Recognize selected.

First recognition downloads a large handwriting model (potentially over 100 MB). This may be slow or fail on restricted Wi-Fi. VPN or mobile data may be required. This experimental model has not yet been validated on your handwriting.

3. Verify pounds and copy grams

Check every number against the photo. An AI guess is never accepted automatically: tap Use suggestion or type the correct number.

Tare is treated as positive. Product total = (product lb + tare lb) × 453.59237. Round only at the end.
